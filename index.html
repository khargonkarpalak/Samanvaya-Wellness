About Us

About Samanvaya Wellness

Integrating Science, Nature & Lifestyle

01 · Our Story

Wellness that fits everyday life

Samanvaya Wellness was envisioned as a platform to bring together health education, individual wellness support and practical lifestyle approaches.

We believe that wellness should be accessible, understandable and relevant to everyday life. Our aim is to help individuals and communities develop a better understanding of health and make informed choices that support long-term well-being.

Through consultations, educational programmes, institutional initiatives and wellness resources, we encourage a balanced approach to healthier living.

02 · What Samanvaya Means

Harmony, coordination and integration

Samanvaya represents harmony, coordination and integration.

Our name reflects the belief that well-being involves multiple interconnected aspects of life. By bringing together scientific understanding, natural wellness practices and sustainable lifestyle choices, we aim to create a more integrated approach to health.

03 · Mission & Vision

What we work towards

Our Mission

To make evidence-informed wellness knowledge and practical lifestyle education accessible to individuals and communities through integrated health approaches.

Our Vision

To become a trusted platform for wellness, health education and knowledge sharing, contributing to healthier individuals and more health-conscious communities.

About Dr. Palak

Naturopathy, yoga and integrative health

With qualifications in Bachelor of Naturopathy and Yogic Sciences (BNYS), Master of Science in Yoga (MSc Yoga) and Diploma in Acupressure Healing Sciences (DAHS), her professional interests span naturopathy, yoga therapy, nutrition, lifestyle management and traditional therapeutic approaches, including acupressure and acupuncture.

As an educator in naturopathy and yogic sciences, she is actively involved in teaching, student mentoring, health education and academic initiatives. Her professional journey has also nurtured an interest in clinical practice, interdisciplinary learning and the development of practical wellness approaches.

Her areas of interest include preventive healthcare, metabolic and women's health, therapeutic yoga, nutritional guidance, lifestyle modification and the responsible integration of traditional wellness practices with contemporary health knowledge.

Professional Journey & Experience

Clinic, classroom and community

With over five years of experience in the field of naturopathy, yoga and wellness, Dr. Palak Khargonkar's professional journey brings together clinical exposure, academic teaching, wellness consultations and institutional engagement.

Through practical training experiences across different naturopathy and wellness settings, she has gained exposure to various approaches to naturopathy, yoga and lifestyle-based wellness.

Alongside her academic role as an educator in naturopathy and yogic sciences, she has also worked as a Yoga Consultant with HealthifyMe, extending her professional experience into individual wellness guidance and yoga-based lifestyle support.

Her academic and institutional contributions include student mentoring, organising educational workshops and health awareness programmes, coordinating innovation-related initiatives and supporting student-led projects.

With a keen interest in research and academic writing, she has pursued advanced training in biomedical research and biostatistics and contributed to published book chapters.

Her professional journey continues to be guided by an interest in connecting traditional wellness practices with contemporary health knowledge and making evidence-informed approaches to well-being more accessible.

Health Disclaimer

Content on this website is for general education and wellness. It does not replace diagnosis or treatment by a qualified doctor.

  • For diagnosed conditions, our support complements standard medical care.
  • We do not promise cures or guaranteed results.
  • Do not stop or change prescribed medication, including insulin, without your doctor.
  • Some practices, like fasting, yoga, heat or cold therapy and massage, are not suitable for everyone. Check with your doctor if you are pregnant or have a medical condition.

In an emergency, contact your doctor or nearest hospital, or call 112.
